Public safety on the same engine

BitVoice also carries a 911 answering-point profile, a P25 radio dispatch console and NG911 Core Services. They stay off the menu until Public-safety profiles (lab) is switched on in Settings.

Not certified, not yet live. These profiles have not run against a live ESInet, P25 RFSS or PSAP CAD, and are not certified for live 911 or radio dispatch. They are tested against a real ISSI capture, NENA sample data and stand-ins. Office 911 (Kari's Law, RAY BAUM'S Act locations) is part of the phone system, not this track.

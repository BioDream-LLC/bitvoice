How it rings

Woken for calls, then straight to BitVoice

When a call comes in, BitVoice sends a push (Firebase on Android, Apple's VoIP push on iPhone) that carries only the call's ID, who is calling and your BitVoice's address. The phone shows the call on its own call screen and connects to your BitVoice for the sound. Ring groups and queues ring the app together with the extension's other phones. The Android app released today has no push yet: it rings while it is running, in the background too, and is not woken once Android closes it.

  • Answer from the lock screen or a Bluetooth headset
  • Calls you make go on the phone's call system too (Telecom, CallKit), so Bluetooth and the phone know of them
  • Hold shows on the phone's call system, and on iPhone it works from the call screen too
  • Calls keep going with the screen off or another app open
  • Moving from Wi-Fi to mobile data keeps the call (ICE restart)
